# New This Week

This file is the rolling discovery log for newly identified repositories, applications and experiments.

## Review workflow

For each candidate:

1. Confirm that the repository/tool is genuinely relevant to oil & gas or subsurface work.
2. Check activity, documentation, license and technical depth.
3. Assign a workflow category and maturity level.
4. Flag emerging technology signals.
5. Add accepted projects to `projects.csv`.
6. Move especially interesting projects to `EMERGING_PROJECTS.md`.
